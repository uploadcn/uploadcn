# Code of Conduct

UploadCN follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, and focus on what is best
for the project and the people using it. Harassment, personal attacks and
discrimination are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/uploadcn/uploadcn/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches maintainers only), or message a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban participants who
break this code.
